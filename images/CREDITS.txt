Using your own image instead
----------------------------
Better. Use a photo you took, or something you made.
If you take one from the internet, check the license first and credit it in your footer.
Put the file in an images folder next to your index.html, then point at it:

  <img src="images/your-file.jpg" alt="what the picture shows">

Every image needs alt text that says what the picture SHOWS,
not what it is. Not "image", not "photo1.jpg".
